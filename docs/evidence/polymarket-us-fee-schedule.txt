Polymarket US Fee Schedule — retained primary-source extract
===========================================================

Source: https://docs.polymarket.us/fees   (page title: "Trading Fee Schedule";
description: "Trading fee schedule, rebates, and examples")
Retrieved / re-verified: 2026-09-06 (server-rendered HTML of the official docs
site; the page fetches cleanly, no bot challenge).

Verbatim — effective date
-------------------------
"Effective exchange-wide from 12 AM ET, Wednesday July 1, 2026."

Verbatim — fee formula
----------------------
"Fees are computed using a symmetric formula that scales with price uncertainty:

  Fee = Θ × C × p × (1 - p)

Where:
  - C is the number of contracts
  - p is the trade price ($0.01 to $0.99)
  - Θ (theta) is the fee coefficient"

Coefficient table (verbatim, single exchange-wide row)
-----------------------------------------------------
                | Theta   | Max (p = $0.50)
  Taker Fee     |  0.06   |  $1.50
  Maker Rebate  | -0.0125 | -$0.31

"Maker rebate is applied at the point of trade."
Volume-tiered TAKER rebate (retrospective, paid weekly), prior calendar-month
taker volume: $250k-$999,999 -> 10%; $1M-$9,999,999 -> 25%; $10M+ -> 50%.

Verbatim — rounding
-------------------
"All fees and rebates are rounded to the nearest $0.01 using banker's rounding
(round half to even). For example, $0.025 rounds to $0.02 (down to even), while
$0.035 rounds to $0.04 (up to even)."

Verbatim — misc
---------------
"Fees are only charged when a trade executes."
"On small trades (low quantity or prices near $0.00 or $1.00), the fee can round
down to $0.00."

Verification: taker fee = banker_round_cent(0.06 * C * p * (1 - p)) reproduces
the "Taker Pays" column for every row above (note $0.05: 0.06*100*0.05*0.95 =

UNRESOLVED — "0.05 taker coefficient" claim (see docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md A-025)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
A task prompt asserted the current official Polymarket US taker coefficient is
0.05. This is NOT supported by the primary source: the live docs.polymarket.us
"Trading Fee Schedule" page above, dated effective July 1, 2026, shows a single
exchange-wide Taker Fee Θ = 0.06 with a $1.50 max, and its own worked "Fee
Schedule by Price" table is internally consistent with Θ = 0.06.

Several third-party sites (evidence-hierarchy level 5) describe a July-2026
CATEGORY-specific theta split — e.g. sports 0.05, tech/mentions 0.04, crypto
0.07, geopolitical/economic 0.00 — and a separate March-2026 CFTC filing has
been reported as a basis-points-of-"Total Contract Premium" schedule (0.30%
taker / 0.20% maker rebate). None of these appears on the official docs page.
The model in code remains at the primary-source value Θ_taker = 0.06 until a
primary source states otherwise; any category/series-specific coefficient a
caller uses must itself be evidence-backed.
